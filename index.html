Check your progress on the Deluge

Save your in-progress patch to the SD card on the Deluge (any name, anywhere under /SYNTHS). Then click Check on device — you'll pick that file once, and every click after just re-reads it.

The steps below update automatically: a step ticks itself off once it's a perfect match, turns yellow if you've changed it but it's not there yet, and red if you changed something this preset doesn't actually use. Check settings lets you loosen or tighten how exact a match needs to be.
